Design a mobile fintech app home screen in a dark glassmorphism style.

BACKGROUND:
- Deep dark navy/black background with soft blurred gradient blobs
  (teal/cyan #00D4D4 and deep blue #1A2A6C) glowing behind the UI.
- Subtle noise/grain texture for depth. The blobs slowly drift and
  pulse (20s loop).

GLASS CARDS (Notifications, Create "My home", All services, Monitoring, Transfer without fees):
- Large corner radius (28-32px).
- Background: rgba(255,255,255,0.06) with backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(140%).
- 1px inner border rgba(255,255,255,0.12), top edge slightly brighter
  to simulate light hitting glass.
- Soft shadow: 0 10px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.4).
- Small line icon in the top-left, bold white label at the bottom-left.
- Some cards are partially cut off on the right edge (horizontal scroll).

ACTION BUTTONS (Pay, Transfer, payme go):
- Pill shape, solid white / light frosted fill, dark text, icon on the left.
- Soft white glow: box-shadow 0 0 30px rgba(255,255,255,0.35).
- Hover/press: scale 0.96 with a spring animation, glow intensifies.

ACCENT:
- Cyan/teal highlight color for icons, the "+" badge and small details,
  with a faint neon glow (drop-shadow 0 0 12px #00E5FF).

BOTTOM:
- Light blue rounded sheet sliding up from the bottom with large bold
  headline text.
- Floating pill button "Transfer to" with a heavy blur backdrop
  (blur 30px), white fill and a long soft shadow.

HEADER:
- "Your balance" in small gray text, "Balance hidden" in large bold white
  with an eye-off icon; avatar circle with initials top-right,
  glass style.

ANIMATIONS:
- On load: cards fade in and rise 20px with a stagger of 60ms each
  (ease-out, spring).
- Buttons: tap squish + glow pulse.
- Background blobs: slow floating motion.
- Cards: on scroll, a slight parallax and blur change.
- All transitions 250-400ms, cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1).

Typography: modern geometric sans-serif (Inter / SF Pro / Manrope),
heavy weight for headings.
Output: HTML + CSS + JS (single file), mobile viewport 390x844.
